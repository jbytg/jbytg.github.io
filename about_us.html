Safety First

SECURITY & ACCOUNTS

Passwords handled by Firebase. Your password is checked by Firebase Authentication and is never stored in the JB HUB database.
Security check. After 3 wrong password tries a quick check appears to keep accounts safe from guessing.
Email verification. Firebase sends a verification link to your email; open it, return and check your status.
App Check protection. Requests to the database are guarded so only the real JB HUB app can talk to it.
Works on tricky networks. A compatibility mode helps on networks and phones that block normal real-time connections.
You stay in control. Log out safely any time, or delete your account from the Danger Zone in Account.

FREQUENTLY ASKED

Everything you need to know about JB HUB.

Is JB HUB completely free to use?
Yes, 100%! All core workspace features, arcade games, and live chat features are completely free with zero ad interruptions.
How is my data protected?
Your sessions and cloud notes are handled securely using Google Firebase real-time database, hosted reliably on GitHub Pages.
Who built and hosts JB HUB?
JB HUB was architected and developed by Johan Biju, hosted on GitHub Pages, and synced via Google Firebase.
Which games can I play?
JB HUB has 11 arcade games: Cyber Snake, Memory Match, Flappy Bird, Breakout, 2048, Glitch Hunter, Neon Dodger, Space Invaders, Cyber Runner, Stack Tower and Neon Racer. Each one has its own leaderboard.
How long do chat messages stay?
Global Chat is public and clears automatically every 24 hours. You can delete your own messages at any time.
The site feels laggy. What can I do?
Open Settings from the three-dot menu and switch to Low graphics mode. It removes blur and animations for the smoothest experience on slower phones.
What are JB TV and JB GUARD?
They are extra tools in the Tools section. JB TV lets you watch 10,000+ free channels, and JB GUARD helps secure your accounts. Both open in a new tab.
What happens to my password?
Your password is checked by Firebase Authentication and is never stored in the JB HUB database. After 3 wrong tries a quick security check appears.
Does it work on my phone?
Yes. Every game has touch controls, the layout adapts to small screens, and Low and Medium graphics keep it smooth on phones.
I found a bug or have an idea.
Open Bug & Suggest from the menu and send it in. Every report helps make JB HUB better.
