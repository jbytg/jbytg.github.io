Institutional Overview

Welcome to MGD HSS for Boys Kundara

MGD HSS for Boys Kundara is a respected boys' educational institution situated in Kundara, Kollam district, Kerala. The school is organized into two dedicated administrative and academic divisions:

1. Upper Primary & High School Section (Classes 5 to 10): Providing strong foundational education in UP (Classes 5–7) and comprehensive secondary education in High School (Classes 8–10), where students also participate in grace-mark eligible clubs and uniformed units.

2. Higher Secondary Section (Plus One & Plus Two): Offering focused Plus One and Plus Two education in Bio Science and Commerce with Computer Application, alongside NSS and Souhritha Club initiatives.

Our Legacy Since 1916

A Century of Educational Heritage

MGD HSS for Boys, Kundara has a proud educational tradition spanning more than a century. Its journey reflects a lasting belief in education as a powerful foundation for progress.

1916 · Founding

Founded in a Vision of Progress

Founded in 1916 by H.H. Baselious Geevarghese II Catholicos in sacred memory of his Guru, Vattasseril Mar Dionysius Metropolitan, who envisioned education as the ultimate tool for progress.

2014 · A Major Milestone

Upgraded to Higher Secondary

The school was officially upgraded to a Higher Secondary School in 2014. The Plus Two courses were formally inaugurated on 9 October 2014 by Sri Ramesh Chennithala.

Today · Management

A Continuing Educational Mission

The school functions under the benevolent management of the Catholicate and M.D. Schools Corporate Management. The current manager is H.G. Zachariah Mar Severios Metropolitan.

Our Educational Philosophy

Shaping Character, Nurturing Potential

Our educational philosophy centres on noble character formation and holistic personality development. We aim to equip students with the knowledge, confidence and values needed to face the challenges of the digital era.

Campus Organization

Our Two School Sections

Section 01 · Classes 5 – 10

UP & High School Section

Covers Upper Primary (Classes 5–7) and High School (Classes 8–10) under the Kerala SCERT curriculum.

  • UP Wing (Classes 5–7): Focused on core classroom academics, reading, foundational science, mathematics and arts without external camps or special club enrolments.
  • High School Wing (Classes 8–10): Dedicated club and uniformed unit activities (NCC, SPC, JRC, Little KITEs) begin in Class 8 and conclude in Class 10, offering eligible SSLC grace marks.
Section 02 · Classes 11 & 12

Higher Secondary Section (HSS)

Operates as a separate Higher Secondary wing for Plus One (+1) and Plus Two (+2) students under DHSE Kerala.

  • Two Specialized Streams: Bio Science and Commerce with Computer Application.
  • Dedicated HSS Units: National Service Scheme (NSS), Souhritha Club and Career Guidance and Counselling Cell, supporting Plus One and Plus Two learners.

Higher Secondary Streams

Our Higher Secondary wing offers two focused career-oriented streams following the Kerala DHSE curriculum, supported by dedicated laboratories and experienced faculty.

Higher Secondary (+1 & +2)

Bio Science Stream

Ideal for students aiming for careers in medicine, allied health sciences, biotechnology, engineering, agriculture and pure sciences.

  • Physics, Chemistry, Biology & Mathematics
  • Hands-on practical sessions in dedicated Science Labs
  • Strong conceptual foundation for NEET, KEAM & CUET
  • Individual academic mentoring and continuous evaluation
Higher Secondary (+1 & +2)

Commerce with Computer Application

Combines core business, finance and economic principles with modern computing skills for commerce and IT-enabled management careers.

  • Accountancy, Business Studies & Economics
  • Computer Application with practical IT Lab training
  • Strong preparation for B.Com, BBA, CA, CMA & CS
  • Practical understanding of digital accounting & business tools
UP & High School Section (Classes 5 – 10)

School Education Stages

Classes 5, 6 & 7

Upper Primary (UP) Wing

Builds strong fundamentals in languages, mathematics, basic science and social studies in a supportive classroom environment. UP students focus purely on classroom learning and school-level arts/sports without external camps or special club enrolments.

Classes 8, 9 & 10

High School (HS) Wing

Prepares students thoroughly for the SSLC board examinations while opening doors to structured co-curricular clubs (NCC, SPC, JRC, Little KITEs) from Class 8 to Class 10 with eligible SSLC grace marks.
